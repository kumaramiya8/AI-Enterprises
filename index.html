Our process

Three steps from building blueprint to commissioned handover

A structured execution lifecycle with documented verification at every milestone.

Step 1

Heat load and duct design

We calculate cooling load using ASHRAE standards based on glass exposure, orientation, and occupancy. Then we coordinate 3D duct layouts to prevent site clashes.

Step 2

Supply and pressure testing

Direct factory procurement of certified OEM equipment. Refrigerant piping is brazed under nitrogen purging and held under 550 PSI pressure test for 24 hours.

Step 3

Commissioning and handover

Our technicians balance airflow across every grille using electronic flow hoods, verify water flow rates, and hand over complete as built documentation with AMC coverage.

Frequently asked questions

Questions about our engineering and contracting

Clear answers on pricing, timelines, equipment warranties, and testing protocols.

Our turnkey scope covers site thermal surveys, computerized heat load calculations, shop drawings, equipment procurement from authorized manufacturers, duct fabrication, piping installation, electrical control wiring, calibrated testing adjusting and balancing, and final handover documentation with staff training.
We follow ASHRAE and ISHRAE computerized simulation methodologies. Calculations account for wall and glass thermal transmittance, solar orientation, lighting wattage, fresh air intake requirements, and peak occupant density rather than arbitrary rules of thumb.
We have direct supply partnerships with Daikin, Voltas, Blue Star, Carrier, Mitsubishi Electric, Trane, and Johnson Controls. All equipment is supplied brand new with manufacturer serialized warranties.
Refrigerant copper piping is brazed under continuous dry nitrogen purging to prevent internal oxidation. Once assembled, the system is held under 550 PSI dry nitrogen pressure for 24 hours to verify zero micro leakage before vacuum evacuation.
TAB is the physical verification of air and water circulation. We measure CFM across every diffuser using calibrated electronic flow hoods and balance water flow across chillers and fan coils to ensure temperatures match design specifications.
